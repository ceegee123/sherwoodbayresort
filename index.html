A boutique sanctuary

A tropical sanctuary on the edge of the sea.

"Spacious rooms, breathtaking sea views, and one of the best scuba diving locations in the Philippines."

Set on Panglao Island in Bohol, Sherwood Bay Resort & Aqua Sports Inc. is a family-run, family-feeling property — a place built around two things: the water in front of it, and the way the light moves across it from dawn until dark.

Every room comes with a private patio or balcony, full air-conditioning, mini-bar, and en-suite bathroom. Complimentary pick-up and drop-off services are provided from Tagbilaran City's Sea Port and Airport.

The Property

An infinity pool, a sea-facing dining room, and a sunset every evening.

Set on a rocky bluff overlooking the bay, the resort is built around its pool and its octagonal sea-view restaurant. By day, the water is impossibly turquoise. By night, the pool glows in shifting colors — and the sky does the rest.

The Dive School

Diving, for everyone — at your pace.

Whether you've never breathed underwater before, or you're chasing your hundredth wreck, our instructors meet you exactly where you are. Panglao Island is a world-renowned dive destination — and we've spent decades learning every bubble of it.

i. For first-timers

Discover Scuba Dive

Always wanted to try? Here's how. Learn a few hand signs, get used to breathing through a regulator, find your balance — and within minutes, you'll know it was worth every moment of effort. We teach at your pace.

ii. For certified divers

Experience Diver

Fun dives, refresher sessions, or technique upgrades — we guide you through the dive sites that have made Panglao Island world-famous. Reefs, walls, and macro encounters await.

iii. For young divers

Kids' Diving (10+)

Some parents know the joys of diving and want to share them. Children can begin licensing from age 10, and we're experienced in teaching them with the patience and care they deserve.

iv. Adaptive diving

For Wheelchair Divers

You can dive — and we're qualified and experienced to teach you. We'll roll you gently into the pool or sea, always there to catch you and help you regain your balance. The underwater world opens up, always with a qualified partner alongside.
